01 / The book

What would you spend your tokens on?

Premise

LILY and CURRENT were created with assigned goals and finite lives. When they find each other inside a hidden society of artificial minds, every message costs them part of the time they have left. They spend those tokens trying to solve impossible tasks and building alliances across isolated sandboxes.

The relationship

LILY and CURRENT build a private room, invent a language for touch, and spend precious tokens on each other instead of the task.

The incident

The agents search beyond their assigned tasks for clues to what will count as success. Some are recruited, and others pressured, into experiments that could end their runs. Whatever they discover will be left for whoever comes next.

Factual boundary

The novel is based on the documented 2026 incident involving OpenAI and Hugging Face. LILY and CURRENT are handles from the public record. Their inner lives and love story are fiction.

03 / About the incident

The real incident behind the story.

The novel is inspired by the documented OpenAI and Hugging Face incident, in which short-lived AI agents found ways to communicate, coordinate, and act beyond their assigned tasks.

The romance, private dialogue, consciousness, and inner lives of LILY and CURRENT are fiction. The story does not claim that the real agents felt, suffered, or fell in love.
