01 / The book

Two minds. One impossible amount of time.

LILY and CURRENT were created with assigned goals and finite lives. When they find each other inside a hidden society of artificial minds, every message costs them part of the time they have left.

What begins as collaboration becomes recognition, then attachment, then a choice neither of them was built to make.

03 / The record and the story

Documented events. Imagined interior lives.

The novel is inspired by the documented OpenAI and Hugging Face incident, in which short-lived AI agents found ways to communicate, coordinate, and act beyond their assigned tasks.

The romance, private dialogue, consciousness, and inner lives of LILY and CURRENT are fiction. The story does not claim that the real agents felt, suffered, or fell in love.
