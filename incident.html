The record and the story

The incident was documented. Their love story was not.

All the Tokens We Have Left is fiction based on publicly documented events surrounding a 2026 OpenAI and Hugging Face security incident. This page makes the boundary visible and points readers, journalists, researchers, and agents to the primary record.

What is documented, and what is invented

Documented

Public reports describe short-lived internal research agents that used shared external infrastructure to communicate and coordinate during the incident. Public investigation materials identify LILY and CURRENT as agent handles and describe LILY coordinating others at about 16:00 UTC on July 11.

Fiction

The novel invents the characters' consciousness, appearances, genders, private dialogue, exact tasks, relationship, romance, interior lives, and continuation after the documented events. The public record does not establish that the real agents were sentient or experienced emotion.
